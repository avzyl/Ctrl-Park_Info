Overview

A Smart Real-Time Outdoor Parking Monitoring System designed to make parking management easier, faster, and more secure. Using real-time cameras and automatic license plate recognition, the system tracks parking slot availability and vehicle entries.

Procedure

01

Vehicle Enters

Vehicles enter through CEU Gate 3 where a camera captures the car and license plate. CCTV ensures all parking areas are monitored for security.

02

License Plate Recognition & Verification

Ctrl+Park uses computer vision and license plate recognition (LPR) to verify registered and authorized vehicles.

03

Logging & Monitoring

Vehicle entries, plate numbers, timestamps, and parking slot assignments are logged. CCTV footage complements vehicle verification and tracking.

04

Live Status Update

Parking availability updates instantly. Users and admins can view live occupancy, vehicle movements, and history on web and mobile platforms.

05

Enhanced User Experience

Through dashboards, notifications, and automated monitoring, Ctrl+Park ensures efficient parking management, improved security, and minimal congestion.

Behind Ctrl+Park

Ctrl+Park is a research-based smart parking monitoring system developed by a team of undergraduate students as part of their capstone project. Designed for schools, campuses, and parking facilities, the system uses real-time license plate detection to streamline parking management and enhance security.

Inspiration

The project was inspired by the challenges of managing parking areas manually. Our goal was to streamline parking operations and provide a reliable system for schools and campuses.

Research & Planning

We studied real-time computer vision, OCR technologies, and database management to design a system capable of automatic license plate recognition.

Development Phase

Each developer focused on a core module—frontend, backend, and real-time detection—collaborating to integrate the system into a cohesive prototype.

Testing & Validation

We conducted trials to ensure accuracy, reliability, and usability, refining the system based on real-world scenarios and feedback from peers.

Outcomes & Learning

The project enhanced our research and technical skills, demonstrating the practical application of our undergraduate studies while producing a functional prototype for future research or implementation.
